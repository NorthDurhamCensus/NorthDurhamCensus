# Audio

What the Winamp window plays. Listed in `config.js` under `winamp.tracks`.

## reaching-out.mp3

"Reaching Out" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com).
**Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 3.0** —
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons and converted from Ogg Vorbis to MP3, because
Safari's Ogg support is patchy while MP3 plays everywhere. Nothing else about
the recording was changed.

CC BY means the attribution above must stay with it. It is repeated in
`NOTICE.md`, and Winamp shows the artist and title while it plays.

## Replacing it

This is placeholder music. It has nothing to do with North Durham, and the
better version of this is a local recording: a musician who gives permission,
or the sound of the Saturday market, the fairgrounds, birdsong on a trail.
That would suit a census of the senses far better than stock instrumental.

To swap it, drop the file in here and edit the `winamp.tracks` list in
`config.js`. Record the permission or licence in this file at the same time.
