# Place photos

Photos for `data/listings.js`. A listing points at one with
`"photo": "photos/<file>.jpg"` (a path inside `data/`), or at a full https
address. North of the Ridges loads them straight from GitHub.

- **Free-licence photos are fine too** (Creative Commons, e.g. from Wikimedia Commons).
  Put "Photographer, licence (resized), what it shows, <link to the file page>" in
  `"photoCredit"`. North of the Ridges turns the link into a "Wikimedia Commons" link.
- **Your own photos, or ones you have permission to use.** Put the
  photographer's name in the listing's `"photoCredit"`.
- **Landscape, about 1600px wide, JPEG.** Smaller is kinder to rural internet.
- **Name files after the place:** `palmer-park.jpg`, `blackstock-general-store.jpg`.
- No photos of people who haven't said yes, and nothing with kids' faces.
- **Logos** are fine when the group says yes. Add `"logo": true` so it shows whole on white, and credit it `"Logo © <group>, used with permission"`. Logos belong to their groups and are **not** covered by this repo's MIT licence.
